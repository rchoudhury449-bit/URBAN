Terms of service

Who we are

Urban is a local service-referral service for Barpeta district, Assam, run by Rahul Choudhury (proprietor). Urban connects customers with independent service providers such as electricians, plumbers and drivers. Urban does not do the work itself and the providers are not Urban's employees.

For customers

  • Sending a request is free. Urban charges the customer no fee.
  • Urban chooses a provider and shares your phone number with that provider only after the provider accepts the job.
  • Confirm the price with Urban before the work starts. You pay the provider directly after the work is done.
  • Providers are independent. They are responsible for the quality and safety of their work. Tell Urban about any problem and we will try to help, but Urban does not guarantee the work.

For service providers

  • A provider must register with true details, and accepts the Urban Provider Agreement.
  • Urban charges the provider a commission of 10% of each job received through Urban, payable within 3 days of the job.
  • A late fee of Rs 20 per day applies after the due date (never more than the overdue commission). If commission stays unpaid for more than 7 days after the due date, the account is suspended automatically. Unpaid dues may be recovered through legal action.
  • Providers must not take Urban customers outside Urban to avoid commission. The agreement explains the consequences.

Payments

Payments made to Urban are commission and fees paid by providers. Online payments, where offered, are processed by Razorpay. Urban does not store card or UPI details.

Changes and law

Urban may update these terms. Changes to a provider's commission or fees apply only after 15 days' notice. These terms are governed by the laws of India. Courts at Barpeta, Assam have jurisdiction.
